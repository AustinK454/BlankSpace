Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Welcome to Blank Space. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Blank Space application.

Blank Space is designed to provide a simple place for creating notes, to-do lists, calendar entries, and reminders.

Information We Collect

Blank Space does not currently require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the application.

The information you enter into Blank Space, such as notes, to-do lists, calendar entries, and reminders, is currently stored locally on your device.

How Your Information Is Used

Information you enter into Blank Space is used to provide the features of the application, such as displaying your notes, organizing your tasks, and showing your calendar entries.

Blank Space does not currently use your notes or other locally stored information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information Stored on Your Device

Blank Space uses your device's local storage to save information such as your notes, reminders, calendar entries, and app settings.

Because this information is stored locally, deleting the application's stored data or uninstalling the application may remove information that has not been backed up elsewhere.

Third-Party Services

Blank Space does not currently require third-party accounts or services to store your notes or personal information.

If third-party services are added to Blank Space in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain what information those services receive and how that information is used.

Children's Privacy

Blank Space is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information handled by Blank Space. Because information entered into the current version of Blank Space is stored locally on your device, you should also use appropriate security measures on your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when features or data practices change.

When important changes are made, the updated policy will be made available through Blank Space.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Blank Space, you can contact the developer using the contact information provided with the application.
